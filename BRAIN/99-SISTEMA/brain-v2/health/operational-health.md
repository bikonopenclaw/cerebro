# Operational Health

Status: foundation candidate.

Operational Health measures gate integrity, not knowledge quality.

Formula:

`Operational Health = passed_operational_contracts / total_operational_contracts`

Operational contracts:

- BRAIN-GIT-*
- BRAIN-AUD-001
- BRAIN-AUD-003
- BRAIN-AUD-004
- BRAIN-AUT-*

Thresholds:

- Dry run pass: 1.00
- Foundation QA pass: 1.00
- Merge/push pass: 1.00

Operational Health must be reported separately from Knowledge Health. A combined score is forbidden.
